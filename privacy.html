NEXORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

NEXORA is a personal knowledge and life archive application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use NEXORA.

Information We Access

NEXORA may request access to information or services that are necessary to provide application features. When Google Sign-In is used, NEXORA may receive basic Google account information authorized by the user, such as account identification information required for authentication.

Google User Data

NEXORA only accesses Google user data that the user explicitly authorizes through Google's OAuth consent process. Google user data is used only to provide the features requested by the user.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed by NEXORA is used to provide and maintain application functionality, including authentication and user-requested features.

Data Sharing

NEXORA does not sell users' personal information or Google user data. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by NEXORA. Users should also protect access to their devices and Google accounts.

User Control

Users can revoke NEXORA's access to their Google Account through their Google Account security settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NEXORA's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be sent to the developer contact email associated with NEXORA.
